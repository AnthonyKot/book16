## PART B — two new find-types (promote into BRIEF.md as 9 and 10)

- **9. The empty commit** — tree identical to its parent. Hits ≥11 repos. Four mechanisms:
  message claims absent work (git `e90a4c0e`), conversion drops the payload (cpython svn:ignore),
  deliberate ceremony/section headings (bitcoin SegWit, go release-branches, openssl QUIC),
  mirror/CI trigger (php `00d8e26d`, t2t). Habit: `diff <commit>^{tree} <commit>~^{tree}`, then
  read the subject as an ops log.
- **10. Signature & tag-object archaeology** — hits ≥10 repos. Independent lessons: annotated ≠
  signed (openssl 1.0.1g Heartbleed release unsigned), signature scope ≠ shipped artifact (xz —
  signed the clean tree, not the tarball payload), tag-only history (bitcoin v0.3.20.2 critical
  fix unreachable from HEAD), `mergetag` preserves a deleted tag's signature (linux), lightweight-
  only absence (go: 0 signed of 71,792 commits), taggers as crisis staffing (log4j2's three
  emergency releases, three signers), backdated tags as policy (bitcoin `noversion`, cpython `2.0`).
  Habit: `git cat-file -p <tag>` — read the tag object, not just the ref.

## PART D — cross-repo theses the selection surfaced

1. Every repo failed the folklore grep (`--grep oops/sorry/hack` = noise everywhere). Method =
   dates, names, trees, `-S`/`-L`/`--all`; NOT message-grepping. Chapter-worthy thesis in itself.
2. The genesis lie is universal (import/plant/fabricate day one in every repo).
3. Registry ≠ git (event-stream, colors): the published artifact and the committed artifact are
   different events. Pin the git tag, diff against what the registry resolved.
4. "The last commit is never a speech" held in every repo (Satoshi's IRC lookup, antirez's pun,
   Bram's typo-commit, Yang Tse's VMS Makefile, Bellard's routed patch).
5. Grok's failure signature is stable: real hashes, tidied transcripts, over-confident causal
   "because". The book's editing discipline is the antidote and is now standing policy.
